# Restaurant artwork

The visual direction follows the seven user-supplied isometric pixel-art references attached to the task on 2026-10-28: smaller adult-proportioned figures, warm timber, cream stone, forest-green upholstery and panelling, brass fixtures, detailed plants and restrained parchment UI. This replaces the previous large-headed illustration direction.

Nine local PNG atlases and a garden backdrop in `public/art/` were created with the built-in image tool using the supplied references. No reference screenshot is used as a game background. The room is rendered by the restaurant simulation.

## Layout and rendering

Characters, diners, actions, idle staff and idle diners each contain four rows of four frames. Diner actions contains eight rows of four: four rear walk cycles followed by four seated eating cycles. Restaurant contains three rows of four props. Decor contains two rows of four fixtures. Total: 132 sprite silhouettes.

`src/art.ts` uses explicit alpha-silhouette bounds and shared animation-row scales. Sprites are sampled without smoothing at display resolution. Walking repeats four complete poses at eight frames per simulation second, independently of route boundaries. Pause and reduced motion are preserved. Idle gestures use fixed resting feet.

`src/interior.ts` renders projected timber boards, tile borders, green panelling, curtains, a china cabinet, menu board, columns, planters and a bench. Textured tables and chairs use ground offsets calibrated to the floor; chairs are mirrored to face their tables. Service counters retain projected geometry for dish alignment. UI controls retain their layout with parchment and brass framing.
