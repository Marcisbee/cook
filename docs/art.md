## Eight-frame locomotion revision

The walk construction follows [Raymond Schlitter’s human walk-cycle tutorial](https://www.slynyrd.com/blog/2024/5/24/pixelblog-50-human-walk-cycle): contact, down, passing and reach poses, then the opposite step. A color-coded original joint guide established the leg sequence before applying the existing costumes. [Aseprite’s animation documentation](https://www.aseprite.org/docs/animation) describes the frame-sequence workflow.

Four additional transparent atlases provide 96 complete walking poses: chef and waiter each have two rows of eight; each diner atlas has four rows of eight (front/rear pairs). Rear NW views are mirrored for NE; front SE views are mirrored for SW. Every row shares a scale and ground baseline. Chest registration avoids centering on the swinging leg silhouette. Full cycles advance over 1.6 world tiles of travel, retaining phase through turns and stopping with the actor. These replace the old four-frame walk playback; existing action and idle sheets remain in use. No runtime limb deformation is used.
